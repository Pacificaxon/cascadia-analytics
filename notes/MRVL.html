Executive summary

We initiate coverage of Marvell Technology with a Buy rating. Marvell is the premier pure-play on AI data center networking, holding a duopoly with Broadcom in PAM4 optical DSPs and rapidly scaling a custom ASIC business for hyperscalers.

At its October 6 Investor Day, management guided FY2027 revenue to $12B (up 47%), FY2028 to $20B (up 67%), and set a FY2031 target of $70-90B. Twenty-one analysts raised price targets; JPMorgan went to $360, RBC to $425.

Our six-year DCF (FY2027-2032) models $12B to $80B revenue (midpoint of the $70-90B target), with EBIT margins expanding from 32% to 38%. Using live CAPM (1.50 normalized beta, 5.1% 10-year, 3.7% ERP), we get 10.0% average WACC and a $387 base-case target, 69.7% above spot.

The key insight: at $228, the market implies 36.2% revenue CAGR, well below our 46% base case and the company's 55-60% implied target. The market is underpricing Marvell's own plan.

Our read: This is the most asymmetric setup in our semiconductor coverage. The Investor Day guidance is unprecedented in scale and specificity, backed by 21 analyst raises. At $228, you are paying for 36% growth while the company guides to 55-60%. Even our bear case ($242) is above spot.
