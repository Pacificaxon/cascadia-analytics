Executive summary

We initiate coverage of Lam Research with a Hold, Valuation Concerns rating. Lam is the market leader in wafer fabrication equipment for etch and deposition, critical steps in 3D NAND and advanced logic manufacturing. Q4 FY2026 was a record: $6.72B revenue (up 30%), 51.7% gross margin, 37.4% operating margin.

The business is firing on all cylinders. The stock has run far ahead. Our six-year DCF (FY2026-2031) models 13.5% revenue CAGR in the base case, with EBIT margins expanding from 36.5% to 41.5%. Using live CAPM (1.50 normalized beta decaying to 1.20, 5.1% 10-year, 3.7% implied ERP), we get a 10.0% average WACC and a $152 base-case target, 42.4% below the $264 spot.

The reverse DCF is stark: at $264, the market implies 33.8% revenue CAGR, nearly double our 17% bull case. This is euphoric pricing.

Our read: Lam's fundamentals are excellent, but the 33.8% implied CAGR is disconnected from the cyclicality of wafer fab equipment. Memory capex is strong today, yet the stock prices in a permanent supercycle. We see significant downside risk if WFE normalizes.
