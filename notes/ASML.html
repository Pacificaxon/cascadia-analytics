Executive Summary

We initiate coverage of ASML Holding with a Hold, Valuation Concerns rating. ASML is the sole supplier of EUV lithography systems, the critical bottleneck for advanced semiconductor manufacturing. Q3 2026 was a record quarter: revenue of $11.7B (up 55% year-over-year), 57.5% gross margin, and $5.0B net income. Management raised full-year guidance for the third time, now expecting $44-45B revenue (up 36%).

The business is exceptional. The valuation is demanding. Our six-year DCF (FY2026-2031) models 14.0% revenue CAGR in the base case, with EBIT margins expanding from 38% to 42% on operating leverage. Using live CAPM (1.30 beta decaying to 1.00, 5.1% 10-year, 3.7% implied ERP), we get a 9.4% average WACC and a $1,415 base-case target, 13.8% below the $1,642 spot price.

The key insight is the reverse DCF: at $1,642, the market implies 21.9% revenue CAGR over six years, above our 18.5% bull case. The market is pricing in perfection.

Our read: ASML is the best business in semiconductors, but the stock has run ahead of fundamentals. The Q3 beat and triple-raised guidance are real, yet the 21.9% implied CAGR leaves no room for disappointment. We would be buyers on a meaningful pullback toward our $1,415 base case.

Bull / Bear / Verdict

Bull case

If AI datacenter capex sustains and High-NA EUV ramps faster than expected, ASML exceeds $48B by 2031 with 46% EBIT margins. The monopoly pricing power supports premium multiples. Worth $1,780, or 8% above spot.

Bear case

Semiconductor cyclicality returns, China restrictions tighten, and memory capex pauses. Revenue reaches only $28B by 2031, margins stall at 38%. Worth $1,161, or 29% below spot.

Verdict

We rate ASML Hold, Valuation Concerns. The business quality is unmatched, but at $1,642 the market prices in growth above our bull case. The risk/reward is unfavorable at current levels. We would upgrade to Buy on weakness toward $1,415.

What would change our mind

  • Multiple compression: forward P/E contracting from 35x toward 25x on rate fears would bring the stock to our target without fundamental deterioration.
  • China resolution: easing of export restrictions would unlock $5B+ of deferred demand.
  • High-NA delay: pushout of next-gen EUV would impair the 2028-2030 growth trajectory.
