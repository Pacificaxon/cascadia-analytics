Top Risk Factors (Item 1A)

1. Credit Cycle in the Loan Book: Loans outstanding grew 62.5% year on year to $11.1B. The 90+ day NPL ratio held at 1.0% in benign conditions, and the book has not been tested by a regional downturn. Provisions already scale with growth and would rise sharply if unemployment or FX stress hits Indonesia or Brazil borrowers. [6-K]

2. EBITDA Margin Compression: Group adjusted EBITDA margin compressed to 11.8% in Q2 2026 from 15.8% a year earlier as Shopee reinvested in logistics and marketing. If competitive intensity from TikTok Shop and Temu persists, the margin recovery embedded in the base case arrives later than modeled. [6-K]

3. Competitive Intensity in Commerce: Shopee leads across Southeast Asia, but TikTok Shop is scaling social commerce, Temu is pressing on price, and MercadoLibre defends Latin America. Share defense spending is a recurring claim on the commerce margin pool. [Cons]

4. Garena Single-Franchise Concentration: Garena results still rest heavily on Free Fire. Bookings growth has already normalized from 51.1% in Q3 2025 to 15.5% in Q2 2026, and a franchise fade would remove the cash engine that funds group reinvestment. [6-K]

5. Regulatory and FX Exposure: Sea operates under payments, lending, and e-commerce regulation across Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore, Taiwan, and Brazil. Local currency depreciation flows straight into reported USD results, and lending caps or payments rules can change unit economics quickly. [20-F]
